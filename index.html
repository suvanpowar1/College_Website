About Us

A Legacy of Excellence

Established with the vision of creating paradigm shifts in engineering education, D.J. Sanghvi College of Engineering has consistently ranked among the top engineering colleges. We provide a rigorous academic environment coupled with strong industry connections.

Our Mission

To foster an intellectual and ethical environment in which both skill and spirit will thrive so as to impart high-quality education.

Our Vision

To be recognized globally for engineering education and research leading to well-qualified engineers who are innovative.

Message from the Principal

"Education is not just about academics, but about holistic development. We nurture our students to become critical thinkers and industry-ready professionals."
